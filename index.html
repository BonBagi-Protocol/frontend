How it works

  1. 01

    Members sign a charter.

    Every member's shielded address and share, signed by every member with their own key. Change one digit and every signature breaks.

  2. 02

    The buyer pays once.

    The pay page checks every signature in the browser, then builds one ZIP-321 request: one QR, one shielded transaction.

  3. 03

    Zcash splits it.

    One private output per member, each with its own encrypted memo. Nobody holds the money in between.
