// what the buyer sees: every share

the split

October 2026

A collective sells one thing. A class, a ticket, a commission. Three mentors made it together, so three people should be paid.

Usually one of them collects the money and pays the others later. That person becomes a treasurer nobody elected, holding money that is not theirs, while everyone else waits and trusts.

BonBagi removes that person.

First, the members write a charter: every member's shielded address and their share, signed by each member with their own key. Change one digit and every signature breaks.

Then the buyer pays once. The page checks every signature right there in the browser and builds a single Zcash payment request. One QR code. One transaction.

The buyer sees exactly where the money goes before paying. There is no account to create and nothing to install besides a Zcash wallet.

That one transaction already carries the split: one shielded output per member, each with its own encrypted memo. Nobody holds the money in between.

And nobody outside the deal sees who was paid, or how much. Not the internet. Not us. BonBagi has no server to see it with.

Pay once. Split for all.
