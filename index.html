How it works

Charter → pay → split.

  1. 01Members sign a charter

    Each member's shielded address and share, signed by every member with their own key. Change one digit and the signatures break.

  2. 02The buyer pays once

    The pay page checks every signature in the browser, prints the receipt and builds one ZIP-321 request: one QR, one transaction.

  3. 03Zcash splits it

    The transaction has one shielded output per member, each with a private memo. Nobody holds the money in between.
